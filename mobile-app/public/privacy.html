Privacy Policy

Cognitive Inbox · Last updated: July 7, 2026

Cognitive Inbox ("the app") is a personal note-taking app that uses AI to organize what you capture. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and your choices. The short version: your notes are yours, we don't sell or share data, there are no ads or tracking, and the only third party that processes your note content is Google (Gemini), which powers the AI features.

Data we collect

DataPurposeWhere it's stored
Email address & passwordYour account. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes — we cannot read them.Our database (Neon Postgres, hosted on AWS infrastructure)
Notes you capture — text, voice recordings, photosThe core function of the app: storing and organizing your thoughts.Our database; media files in Vercel Blob storage
AI-derived data — summaries, categories, tags, action items, emotional toneOrganizing your notes automatically.Our database, linked to your account

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or browsing data. There are no third-party tracking SDKs in the app.

How AI processing works

When you capture a note, its content (text, audio, or image) is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the summary, category, tags, and — for voice notes — the transcription. This processing is governed by Google's API terms. We send only the note content itself, never your email or identity.

Device permissions

The app asks for microphone (voice notes), camera and photo library (visual notes) access. Each is used only at the moment you capture, and only for the note you're creating. You can decline; text capture works without them.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone. The only parties that touch your data are our infrastructure providers, acting on our instructions: Vercel (hosting), Neon (database), and Google (AI processing as described above).

Data retention & deletion

Your notes stay until you delete them — deleting a memo in the app removes it from our database. You can delete your entire account and all associated data directly in the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent — your account, notes, AI-derived data, and uploaded media are all removed.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Authentication uses short-lived signed tokens stored in your device's secure keychain. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text.

Children

Cognitive Inbox is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We'll update this page when the policy changes and revise the date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: open an issue on our GitHub project page.

隐私政策

Cognitive Inbox · 最后更新：2026 年 7 月 7 日

Cognitive Inbox（"本应用"）是一款使用 AI 整理笔记的个人应用。本政策说明应用处理哪些数据、数据的去向以及您的选择。 简而言之：您的笔记属于您自己；我们不出售或共享数据；应用内没有广告和追踪；唯一处理您笔记内容的第三方是 Google（Gemini），用于提供 AI 功能。

我们收集的数据

数据用途存储位置
邮箱地址和密码用于您的账户。密码仅以 bcrypt 哈希形式存储——我们无法读取。我们的数据库（Neon Postgres，托管于 AWS 基础设施）
您记录的笔记——文字、语音、照片应用的核心功能：存储和整理您的想法。我们的数据库；媒体文件存储于 Vercel Blob
AI 生成的数据——摘要、分类、标签、待办事项、情绪基调自动整理您的笔记。我们的数据库，关联到您的账户

我们不会收集分析数据、广告标识符、位置信息、通讯录或浏览数据。应用内没有任何第三方追踪 SDK。

AI 处理的工作方式

您记录笔记时，其内容（文字、音频或图片）会发送至 Google Gemini API，用于生成摘要、分类、标签， 以及语音笔记的文字转写。该处理受 Google API 条款约束。 我们只发送笔记内容本身，绝不发送您的邮箱或身份信息。

设备权限

应用会请求麦克风（语音笔记）、相机和相册（图片笔记）权限。 每项权限仅在您记录笔记的那一刻使用，且仅用于您正在创建的笔记。您可以拒绝授权；纯文字记录不需要这些权限。

数据共享

我们不会向任何人出售、出租或共享您的个人数据。接触您数据的只有按照我们的指示运作的基础设施服务商： Vercel（托管）、Neon（数据库）和 Google（如上所述的 AI 处理）。

数据保留与删除

您的笔记会一直保留，直到您删除为止——在应用内删除笔记即从数据库中移除。您可以直接在应用内删除整个账户及所有相关数据： 设置 → 账户 → 删除账户。删除立即生效且不可恢复——您的账户、笔记、AI 生成数据和上传的媒体文件将全部被移除。

安全

所有传输均经过加密（HTTPS）。身份验证使用短期签名令牌，存储在设备的安全钥匙串中。密码经 bcrypt 哈希处理，绝不以明文存储。

儿童

Cognitive Inbox 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会在知情的情况下收集儿童数据。

政策变更

政策变更时我们会更新本页面并修改顶部的日期。重大变更会在应用的版本说明中注明。

联系我们

关于本政策或您的数据的疑问，请在我们的 GitHub 项目页面提交 issue。
